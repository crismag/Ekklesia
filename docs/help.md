Help — Church Portal
=====================

Purpose
-------
This page explains who to contact for site design, bugs, access permissions, security or usability concerns, and how to raise scheduling questions for leaders and church admins. Keep reports short, include a screenshot and steps to reproduce.

The in-app guide is at **/docs** (including **Maintenance** for admins). Developer and ops notes live in `docs/README.md` and `docs/ops-maintenance.md`.

Site design, bugs, access permissions, security & usability
---------------------------------------------------------
- Who to contact: Cris / Page Admins.
- How to raise it: Send a message using the portal Messenger or send an email to the page admins.
- What to include: page or URL, short description, steps to reproduce, browser/device, and one or more screenshots (annotate if useful).
- What to expect: an acknowledgement from the admins, basic triage (is it a bug, permissions issue, or suggestion), and either a quick fix, a scheduled change, or guidance on workarounds.

Attachments & screenshots
-------------------------
- Attach screenshots directly to your message or email.
- If possible, include:
  - the exact page URL
  - the time and timezone when the issue happened
  - the account or role you were using (example: Leader, Admin, Member)

Scheduling concerns — Leaders and Church Admin
--------------------------------------------
- Who to contact: Leaders and Church Admin (for assignments, conflicts, or role changes).
- How to raise scheduling requests: Use the Scheduling area in the portal (Schedules → select ministry → create or edit slots), or message your ministry leader and CC the Church Admins.
- What to include: ministry name, date/time range, event name, desired roles/positions, and any conflicts or special notes.
