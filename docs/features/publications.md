# Publications

The print studio turns the calendar into something to hand out, pin up or
project: a monthly calendar for the foyer, a Sunday schedule, a year at a
glance. Publications can be printed, saved as PDF, or exported as an editable
PowerPoint calendar.

## Starting a publication

From **Calendar → Print this view**, the studio opens with the calendar's
current dates and layers. Choose what to make:

| Layout | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | The month as a grid |
| Weekly | One week in detail |
| Agenda | A dated list |
| Sunday | A Sunday programme and serving schedule |
| Annual | Twelve months on one sheet (without birthdays) |
| Planner | A ministry planning sheet |

## Shaping it

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Paper | Letter, Legal, Tabloid, A4, A3 |
| Orientation | Automatic, portrait or landscape |
| Fit | **One page** (a month on one page, with "+N more" counts) or **Grow to fit** (every entry, continuing onto further pages) |
| Density and entries | Standard to extra compact; compact, readable or showcase entries |
| Type | Serif, sans, display or monospace, with text size and title style |
| Header and footer | Church name, location, period, document type; printed date, website, page numbers and a footer note |
| Notes | Formatted notes above and below the calendar, editable on the sheet |
| Ink | An ink-friendly option for office printers |

## Themes and backgrounds

Built-in themes — Classic, Editorial, Planner, Celebration, Hearth and Economy
— each suit particular layouts. Twelve **seasonal monthly themes** decorate
monthly and weekly calendars, and **automatic** picks the theme for the month
being printed.

A **background picture** can be uploaded (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at least
1100 × 600 pixels, up to 12 MB). Position it, choose how strongly it shows, and
add a white wash so the calendar stays readable. Each person can keep up to 20
pictures.

## PowerPoint themes

Design a calendar's look in PowerPoint:

1. Download a starter file from the studio.
2. Decorate slide 1 in PowerPoint, keeping the named regions
   `CALENDAR_TITLE`, `MONTH_HEADING` and `CALENDAR_GRID`; `SUBTITLE`,
   `FOOTER`, `LEGEND` and `PRINT_NOTE` are optional.
3. Upload the `.pptx`. Ekklesia fills the regions with calendar data for
   monthly and weekly publications.

Themes can be renamed, replaced with a new version, or archived.
Administrators can share a theme with the whole church. Themes support a
defined set of slide artwork, up to 25 MB.

## Saving and sharing

A publication is a **saved recipe**: its layout, settings, theme and a rolling
period such as *this month*, *next month*, *this quarter* or *this year*. Next
month, open it and it shows next month's dates. Publications can be saved,
saved as a copy, renamed, duplicated and deleted; administrators can share
them with the church.

## Output

| Output | How |
| --- | --- |
| Print or PDF | **Print** opens the browser's print dialog; choose a printer or *Save as PDF*. The PDF follows the chosen layout. |
| Editable PowerPoint | **Export editable PowerPoint** downloads a monthly grid with editable text boxes and day shapes, a member-type key and supported theme artwork — ready for further design or slides. Some built-in decorations are left out. |

The campus selected in the top bar is the campus of the publication. Birthday
entries use preferred or first names with an optional last initial, and ages
are never printed in publications.
