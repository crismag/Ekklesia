# Ministries and serving

Each ministry has a workspace for its team and its serving schedule. Schedulers
and leaders fill serving roles for activity dates, either on a grid or straight
from the calendar.

## The ministry directory

**Ministries** lists the church's ministries with their campus, leaders and
next serving date, filtered by the active campus. Administrators create,
rename, move, activate or deactivate ministries (inactive ones are hidden from
everyone else) and set the ministries page's title and subtitle.

## A ministry's workspace

| Tab | What it shows | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | The ministry at a glance | Everyone |
| Members & leaders | The team, its leaders and each member's positions | Schedulers, leaders and administrators in scope |
| Serving roles | The roles the ministry fills on a serving date, and who can fill them | Schedulers, leaders and administrators in scope |
| Schedule | The next eight weeks of assignments, read-only | Everyone |

Three things are kept apart:

- **Membership** — who belongs to the ministry. A member can be marked as the
  ministry's leader; this is a label, not access.
- **Positions** — free-text titles a member holds (up to 20 per person), set by
  leaders and administrators.
- **Serving assignments** — who fills which role on which date.

## Filling the schedule

### The serving grid

**Schedules** opens a ministry's serving grid: activity dates down one side,
serving roles across the other, from a week ago to four weeks ahead by default.

1. Choose the date range and the activities to include.
2. Add people to role cells; a cell can hold more than one person.
3. Save the changes as one batch.

The grid marks problems as you work:

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ⚡ | The person is already serving at an overlapping time elsewhere |
| ≡ | The person already has another role on the same date |
| Clashes filter | Shows only the assignments with a conflict |
| Unfilled count | How many role cells in the visible range are still empty |

Conflict marks are warnings; the grid still lets you save. Names typed in that
do not match a person record get no conflict checks.

### From the calendar

Opening a date on the calendar shows each activity's serving roles and how
many are unfilled. People who can schedule fill or change a role right there;
the change is saved the same way as on the grid.

### Rosters

**Rosters** post schedules that are not tied to an event, such as a weekly duty
list by weekday or a set of dated slots. Rosters appear as their own calendar
layer.

## My schedule and availability

**My schedule** lists the dates and roles you are serving.

**Availability** records the date ranges you cannot serve, with an optional
reason. Schedulers, leaders and administrators can see availability and, for
people they schedule, edit it. The serving grid does not yet warn about
unavailable dates, so check availability when planning.

Ekklesia does not send serving invitations or reminders, and assignments do
not have an accept or decline step; share the schedule through the calendar,
My schedule or a printed publication.

## Printing a ministry schedule

**Print ministry schedule** prints every ministry's assignments for one date on
a single sheet, balanced into three columns — the Sunday handout for the
serving teams. For monthly and weekly calendars, use
[Publications](publications.md).
