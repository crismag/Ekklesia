# Events and the calendar

Ekklesia keeps the church's activities and shows them, with serving, birthdays
and holidays, on one calendar.

## Creating an activity

One shared editor is used from **Events → New**, an event's edit page, and a
date on the calendar.

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Basics | Name, date, start and end time or all day, repeat, until date |
| Where and for whom | Campuses, ministry |
| Add details | Held at (a campus or a named venue with its address), description |
| More options | Event type, tags, whether ministries can assign people to serve |

Schedulers, leaders and administrators create and edit activities within their
campus scope.

## Repeating activities

| Repeat | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Once | A baptism Sunday |
| Weekly or every two weeks | Sunday services, a fortnightly prayer night |
| Monthly by date | The 15th of each month |
| Monthly by weekday | The first Saturday, or the last Friday |
| Selected dates | A list of chosen dates |

A series ends on an until date or after a number of dates.

## Working with one date of a series

Every date of a repeating activity can be handled on its own:

- **Change the time** for this date, this and following dates, or all dates.
- **Change the title or description** for one date only.
- **Reschedule** a single date, or **cancel** it and **restore** it later.
- **Delete** upcoming dates, all dates, or selected dates.

Cancelling and deleting dates is open to schedulers and administrators;
leaders edit activities but do not cancel or delete their dates.

## Who sees an activity

Each event type carries an audience: **public**, **members** or **leaders**.
Leader activities (ministry and leadership meetings) are shown only to leaders
and administrators — in lists, on the calendar and when opened directly — and
only public activities appear in the public feed. An activity without a type
is treated as a members activity.

## The calendar

| View | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Month | The whole month at a glance |
| Agenda | A list of what is coming (the default on phones) |
| Week | A detailed week |
| Day | One day, with its serving roles |

**Layers** turn kinds of information on and off: each event type, serving
assignments, ministry schedules, rosters, birthdays, anniversaries, holidays,
and your own **Others** calendars. Your choice of view is remembered in your
browser.

Selecting a date opens its **day panel**, with each activity, its serving
roles and unfilled count; people who can schedule fill roles there, and people
who can create activities add one for that date.

### Holidays

Administrators add holiday calendars by country or region and sync them; six
years of dates are stored with the application, so the calendar never waits
for an outside service. Each holiday calendar can be hidden or shown as a
layer.

### Saved views

A **saved view** stores a set of layers and, optionally, a screen view, so a
leader can return to "Sunday serving" or "Leadership month" in one step.
Views are private to their owner; administrators can share a view with the
whole church. The campus is not part of a view; it always follows the campus
selector. Publications use the same saved views.

### Others calendars

**Others** calendars collect existing items by matching their text — for
example, everything mentioning "Youth". They are kept in your browser, so they
are not shared, saved in views or printed.

## Not included

Ekklesia does not publish an iCal (ICS) feed for external calendar apps, and
the repeat options do not include daily or yearly series.
