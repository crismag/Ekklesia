# Filling the serving grid

The serving grid *is* the editor. You fill cells in place for the events listed under **Events being scheduled**. Calendar's day panel can fill one date; this page is for the week.

A line under the toolbar counts empty role cells in the visible range. It does not email anyone.

[Insert Screenshot: The serving grid with the unfilled-role count under the toolbar.]

> **Warning:** The grid only covers the activities listed under *Events being scheduled*. If the date you want is missing, it is almost always because its event is not selected — or does not occur in the dates you loaded — rather than because the slot was lost.

## When to use the editor

Use the serving grid when you need to:

- Fill or change several roles across a week.
- See how many roles are still unfilled in the range you loaded.
- Add a special (external) assignee that is not on the ministry list.

Use **Calendar** (click the date) when you only need this Sunday. **Open serving grid** on that panel brings you here.

## How a cell works

Each cell is one role on one occurrence. Date, ministry and role are not fields you type — they are the column, the page, and the row.

- An empty cell shows *—*.
- Each assigned person is a pill. A cell can hold more than one name.
- **Ministry member** searches people on this team. Pick a name and click **Add**.
- **Special** is for someone who is not on the ministry list. Click **Add ★**.
- **×** on a pill removes that person from the cell.

Nothing is stored until **Save changes** at the bottom of the grid.

[Insert Screenshot: One cell with a name pill, the ministry-member search, and the Special row.]

Past columns are view-only: names as text, no add row, no **×**.

## Conflicts and badges

The grid warns; it does not usually block.

- Adding the same person twice to the **same** cell is refused.
- **⚡** — already scheduled on an overlapping activity. You can still add them.
- **≡** — already in another role on this same occurrence (multi-role). You can still add them.
- **★** — special / non-ministry assignee, matched to a person record.
- **?** — special name that did not match a person, so conflict checks cannot run.

Hints appear under the add row while you type, and as icons on the pill after you add.

> **Warning:** Read the ⚡ / ≡ hint before you click Add a second time. The save will keep a multi-role or busy assignment if you proceed.

## The snapshot

**Schedule snapshot** sits above the grid. It is the same dates in cards, a simple list, or a worksheet. Tick **Hide empty roles** when you only want cells that already have names.

**Edit** on a snapshot row focuses the grid on that one occurrence. **Show all schedules** under the grid clears the focus. Start and End do not change.

The snapshot also shows a few **past** dates for looking back. Those stay read-only.

## Saving

The bar under the grid says *No unsaved changes* or *You have unsaved assignment changes.* **Save changes** stays disabled until there is something to write and you may manage schedules.

- Save writes every dirty cell for the events currently selected — not one row at a time.
- A Sunday-only selection never deletes a Prayer Meeting assignment.
- Load, changing event chips, or leaving the page asks *You have unsaved assignment changes. Discard them?*
- If every occurrence in the window is already past, the grid is view-only and says so.

After a successful save, the names are on Calendar and on each person's **My Schedule**. This page does not send mail.

> **Tip:** **Print ministry schedule** (in the title row) is the consolidated Sunday sheet across ministries. **Posted lists** is the standing-rota tool. Neither is this grid.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Assuming a missing date was deleted.** A date leaves the serving grid when its activity is deselected, not when its assignments are removed. Re-select the activity and the names are still there.
- **Reaching for a posted list to staff a service.** Posted lists are standing rotas with no activity behind them. If the date belongs to something on the calendar, it belongs in the serving grid.
- **Leaving without Save changes.** Pills you just added are only on this screen until you save.
- **Adding an unmatched Special name by accident.** A **?** pill will not get conflict or availability checks. Prefer a ministry member, or a Special name that matches a person.
- **Counting on the unfilled line for “we wanted two Servers.”** It counts empty cells, not how many people you hoped to put in a filled one.
