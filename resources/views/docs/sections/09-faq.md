# Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the things people ask most.

## Getting started

**How do I sign in?**
Use the **Sign in** button in the top bar (or `/login`) and enter your email and
password. New accounts are created by an administrator.

**I forgot my password.**
Ask a portal-wide admin to reset it on the **Users & access** page. They'll set a
temporary one for you to change at next sign-in.

**Why don't I see Administration in my menu?**
It appears only when you are signed in and your account includes administration. It opens the area for
portal-wide admins; scoped users see it but it does nothing, and signed-out
visitors don't see it at all.

## People & families

**How do I add a new person?**
Administration → **Member records** → **+ Add person**. Only the last name is
required. The People tab in the top bar is look-up only. To load a whole campus
from the Hub workbook, use **Maintenance → Member import**.

**How do I back up people or portal data?**
**Maintenance** (portal-wide admins). Choose **Create backup** and/or a
snapshot. Files are private on the server; download them from that page.

**Someone is missing from a list.**
Check the **campus selector** in the top bar. Set it to **All campuses** to see
everyone across locations.

**How do I set a profile photo?**
Admins can set it from a person's profile. Individuals can upload or remove their
own photo from the **Manage my profile** panel on their own public profile.

**Why won't a family delete?**
A family can't be deleted while it still has members. Reassign the members to
another family (or none), or deactivate the family instead.

**Why won't an option (classification, role, member type) delete?**
It's still assigned to people. Reassign everyone using it first, then delete it
on the **Member types** page.

## Ministries, roles & scheduling

**What's the difference between "group role" and "account role"?**
Group role is a person's standing on a ministry team (Member/Teacher/Leader).
Account role is what a login can do (admin/leader/scheduler/member). See
**Roles, Leaders & Members**.

**How do I let a leader build schedules?**
Give their **account** the **leader** role, scoped to their campus and/or
ministry, on **Users & access**.

**A ministry's leaders show as 0 or look wrong.**
Confirm the leaders are tagged **Leader** in **Members & leaders** and that the
campus selector matches their campus.

**Where do I see unfilled roles?**
Click the date on **Calendar** — the right panel counts them for that day. The
serving grid counts them for the range you loaded. Neither sends mail.

**How do I print the month I am looking at?**
Calendar → **Print this view**. That opens the print studio with those layers
and dates. Printables is for roster sheets, event lists and birthdays, not the
wall calendar.

**How do I save the layers I always use?**
Calendar → **Save view…** (signed in). That writes a private view. **Open view…**
reopens yours and any shared ones. Custom calendars stay in this browser and are
not part of a saved view.

**Why isn't an activity in the serving-grid list?**
The list is every activity on this campus that happens in the dates you loaded.
Widen the range if you expected something later. Sunday Service is selected
because it is the campus default, not because it is the only thing allowed.
Birthdays and holidays never appear — they are not activities.

**Do assignment changes save as I type?**
No. Add or remove names in the cells, then **Save changes** at the bottom of
the serving grid. Loading a new date range or leaving the page asks you to
discard unsaved work.

## Availability

**Where did "My Availability" go?**
Availability is no longer a self-service page. It's managed by leaders and
administrators. Tell your ministry leader when you're unavailable.

## Guest sign-ups & RSVP

**How do people sign up or RSVP?**
Share the public sign-up link, or an event RSVP link (both are listed under
**Visitors & RSVPs → Access codes**, in *Public links*). Administrators review
submissions under **Visitors & RSVPs → Visitors**: each registration shows
members it may already be, so you can link it to that person instead of
creating a duplicate. A shared family email or phone alone is only a possible
match.

**What's the admin code for those modules?**
They use a rotating **ChristLikeness + WORD** access code (default one week),
managed under **Visitors & RSVPs → Access codes**.
