# Site & Navigation

The fastest way to learn where everything lives. Ekklesia is organised into
**workspaces** — Home, People & Records, Ministries, Events & Calendar, Serving &
Scheduling, Visitors & RSVPs and Admin — and one **sidebar** lists them on every
page. **You only see the workspaces and pages your account can use.**

## The sidebar

On a computer the sidebar sits down the left of every page. On a phone or a
narrow window, press the **menu** button (top right) and the same list opens as
a panel.

- **Ekklesia / your church** — at the top; returns to Home.
- **Workspaces** — the one you are in is opened out to show its pages, with the page you are on highlighted. Choose another workspace to go to its first page.
- **You** — at the bottom: your name, **Account**, the **User guide** and **Sign out**. Visitors who are not signed in see **Sign in** here.
- **Collapse** — shrinks the sidebar to icons. Your choice is remembered on this device.

| Workspace | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Home, My schedule, My availability, Account |
| **People & Records** | Directory, Member records, Households, Import & export, Record settings |
| **Ministries** | Ministries, Manage members & leaders, Manage ministries |
| **Events & Calendar** | Calendar, Events, New event, Event categories, Calendar settings, Print calendar |
| **Serving & Scheduling** | Schedules, Schedule board, Rosters, Printables |
| **Visitors & RSVPs** | Sign-ups & RSVP, and the guest sign-up form |
| **Admin** | Overview, Users & access, Church information, Campuses, Portal appearance & notices, Backups & maintenance, System |

Visitors who are not signed in see Home, Events & Calendar, and the guest
sign-up form.

## The top bar

- **Where you are** — the workspace and the page.
- **Campus selector** — pins the whole site to one campus, or **All campuses**. This is the *only* campus control; pages no longer carry their own.
- **Search** — people, activities, ministries and pages. An activity found here opens that activity, not the list it came from.
- **Your name** — opens the user menu.

> **Tip:** Most "why can't I see X?" questions come down to the **campus selector**. If a person, ministry or activity seems missing, set it to **All campuses**.

## The words this portal uses

Five words do most of the work. Two of them used to name the same thing
(serving vs a posted list), and **People** is easy to mix up with the admin
records page.

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | Anything on the calendar — a service, a meeting, a holiday, a training night. The Events page lists them and the URL is still `/events`. |
| **Serving** | Who is on the roles of an activity's date. The serving grid is where those names go. |
| **Posted list** | A standing rota with no activity behind it — a cleaning rotation, a pickup run. It is not a second kind of schedule. |
| **Calendar** | Where you look, and where you act on a day. Click a date for what is on it; **Print this view** is the wall calendar of what you are looking at. |
| **People** | The Directory. Look someone up. Adding a record is **Member records** in People & Records, not this page. |

A ministry's page offers **Serving grid** and **Posted lists** as separate
buttons, because they answer different questions.

## The user menu

Click your name in the top right. Everything about *you* is here, whether or not
you administer anything:

- **My profile** — your account, password and details.
- **My schedule** — where you are serving.
- **My availability** — when you cannot serve.
- **Administration** — only if your account includes it.
- **Sign out**.

This works the same on a phone.

## Administration pages

Administration pages live in the workspace their work belongs to — member
records under People & Records, event categories under Events & Calendar — and
each shows its workspace's pages as tabs across the top. A ministry leader who
manages events sees those pages in Events & Calendar and nothing in Admin; if
your account includes no administration at all, an administration page says so
plainly rather than showing a menu that leads nowhere.

**System** is deliberately last and deliberately dull. It holds read-only
troubleshooting information — configuration files, environment details — and
the development roadmap: useful when something is wrong or somebody is helping
you remotely, and at no other time.

## Home

Home is the **church visiting page** — the same public feed for guests and
members:

- **Everyone** sees the welcome rotator, upcoming events, announcements, ministries, and visit/contact information. Signing in is not required.
- **Signed-in members** also get a **For you** overlay: greeting, this week, my schedule and next assignments. It never replaces the public feed.
- **Leaders** also get **Lead a ministry**.
- **Admins** write announcements under **Admin → Portal appearance & notices**.

## Public vs. signed-in

- **Public pages** show a safe, limited view — first name and last initial, no contact details or addresses.
- **Signing in** unlocks contact details and your own schedule.
- What you can *see* and what you can *do* are checked on the server every time, not decided by which links are on screen.
