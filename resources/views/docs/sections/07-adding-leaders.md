# Roles, Leaders & Members

This section explains the two kinds of "role" in the portal and how to make
someone a member or leader of a ministry — and how to give a leader
the access they need.

## Two kinds of role (don't mix them up)

- **Group role** — a person's standing on a ministry team: **Member** or **Leader**. Managed on the ministry's **Members & leaders** tab.
- **Account role** — what a login can do in the portal: **admin**, **leader**, **scheduler**, **member**. Managed in **Users & access**.

> **Tip:** Group role = standing on a team. Account role = permissions for a login. A person can be a **Leader** on a team (group role) without a portal account, and an account can have the **leader** role scoped to a ministry.

## Family roles

Separately, each person has a **family role** within their household —
**Husband**, **Wife** or **Child**. They grant no access; they describe the
household, and the portal fills most of them in for you where the household says
so plainly. See **Families & Households**.

There is deliberately no "head of household": one address can hold more than one
family, and nobody needs nominating as its head.

## Adding a member to a ministry

1. Open **Ministries**, choose the ministry, and open its **Members & leaders** tab.
2. Under **Add someone**, type the person's name, pick them, and keep **Member**.
3. Select **Add**. They now appear on the ministry's roster and can be scheduled.

## Promoting to Leader

1. On the same **Members & leaders** tab, find the person on the team.
2. Select **Make leader**. (**Make member** reverses it.)

**Leaders** are surfaced on the ministry page and are filtered by campus, so a
leader assigned to one campus is shown only under that campus.

## Giving a leader portal access

Being a **Leader** on a team (group role) is a label. To let that leader actually
build schedules and manage their team in the portal, give their **account** the
matching role:

1. Open **Admin → Users & access**.
2. Find the person's login and open it, or **Add a login** (email + password) and link it to their person record.
3. **Add a role** of **leader**, scoped to their **campus** and/or **ministry**.
4. The leader can now manage schedules and people within that scope.

## Choosing a scope

- **No scope** on an *admin* role = portal-wide admin (everything).
- **Campus scope** limits the role to one location.
- **Ministry scope** limits the role to one team.
- Combine both to limit a leader to, say, *Worship at Scarborough*.

## Editing the role options themselves

The list of **group roles** and **family roles** (and member types) is editable
on the **Record settings** page (People & Records). You can rename, reorder, or add options there. An option
that's still assigned to people can't be deleted until they're reassigned.

> **Best Practice:** Keep the number of leaders per ministry small and scoped to the right campus. It keeps schedules clean and access easy to reason about.
