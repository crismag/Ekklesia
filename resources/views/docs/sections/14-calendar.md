# Calendar, views and print

Calendar is where you look at the church week, and where you act on a day. Activities, serving, people and print are views of that same week — not separate apps.

## What you are looking at

The toolbar picks **Month**, **Agenda**, **Week** or **Day**. Agenda is the default on a small screen because a seven-column month is not usable there.

**Sources** in the left rail are the layers on the feed. A layer that is off is off for you in this browser; turning it back on does not change anyone else's calendar.

> **Tip:** Custom calendars ("Others") stay in this browser until a shared settings backend exists. Saving a view does not publish them.

## A day

Click a date — the number, the empty space in a month cell, or **+N more**. The **right** panel opens on that day: what is on it, who is serving, and which roles are still unfilled.

- Members see that read-only.
- Schedulers who may manage schedules can fill or change a role in the panel. The save is the same assignment save the serving grid uses; one role does not wipe the rest of the week.
- **Open serving grid** still takes you to the ministry's week when you need a batch of dates.
- **New event on this day** is offered to people who may create activities. Week and Day views also create from an hour click, because a click there carries a time.

The panel can be collapsed; the month takes the width back. Below a phone-sized screen the collapse control is hidden, same as the left rail.

## Unfilled roles

Empty roles are stated in words. The day panel counts them for that date. The serving grid counts them for the visible range. Neither sends mail; they only show the gap.

## Open view and Save view

Print setup already had named views. Calendar can open the same rows, and a signed-in person can save the layers and screen they are looking at.

- **Open view…** lists private views you own and shared views anyone may reuse.
- Opening applies the layers. If the view also stored month/week/day/agenda or the left rail, those change; if it did not, your current switcher and rail stay put. Paper settings (theme, header, notes) are ignored on screen.
- **Save view…** writes a **private** copy. It does not overwrite a colleague's shared view. To change a shared one, save it as your own, or be its owner or a portal administrator.
- Custom calendars are not part of a saved view.

You have to be signed in. The views API refuses a guest.

## Print this view

**Print this view** opens the print studio with the layers and dates currently on screen. It is a separate document, not this page with print styles over it.

Printables is still the hub for roster sheets, event lists and birthday directories. For a wall calendar of the month you are looking at, use Calendar → **Print this view**.

> **Tip:** A saved publication in the print studio is not the same thing as Month/Week/Day on Calendar. Opening one does not change the other unless the saved row also stored a screen layout.

## Campus

The top-bar campus selector is the only campus control. A saved view does not store campus; the cookie you already chose still applies when you open one.
