# About

The Church Portal is the coordination and member-management tool for
Christlikeness Church. It gives leaders, volunteers, and members a clear,
mobile-friendly way to manage the weekly rhythm of church life.

## What it does

- **People & families** — look someone up on the People tab; add and edit records under **Member records**; households sit next to that in Administration.
- **Ministries & roles** — organize teams, tag leaders, and manage who serves.
- **Schedules & events** — build serving rotations and see what's coming up.
- **Calendar** — a unified view of events, schedules, birthdays, and anniversaries.
- **Guest sign-ups & RSVP** — capture new people and event responses, with duplicate detection against existing members.
- **Administration** — accounts and roles, campuses, church info, site styling, and **Maintenance** (backups, member import/export).

## One place for everything

Member records, families, member types, campuses, church info, photos, and
accounts are all owned by the portal, so leaders and administrators manage the
church's records in a single, consistent place.

## Who it is for

- **Members** — view your schedule and calendar, and manage your own profile.
- **Leaders** — manage your ministry's people, roles, and schedules.
- **Administrators** — run the whole control panel: Member records, families, Members & leaders, accounts, site configuration, and Maintenance.

Start with the topics in this guide. For anything that doesn't behave as
